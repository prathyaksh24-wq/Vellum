# Cheatsheet — Naval's Decision Rules

## Decision Rules

| Situation | Do | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Major life choice, can't decide | Say **no** | Long-lived decisions; options are abundant |
| Two equal choices | **Run uphill** (short-term pain) | Pain path carries long-term gain; compound interest |
| Something bothers you | **Change / Accept / Leave** — never just wish | Aversion is most misery |
| Catching yourself desiring | Ask "will I be unhappy unless this goes my way?" | Most things: no |
| Catching a negative judgment | Ask "what's the positive here?" | Positive interpretation is trainable |
| Fixing a problem under your hourly rate | **Ignore or outsource it** | Time is worth your rate |
| Fixing a problem over your hourly rate | Do it yourself | Same math |
| Someone talks about how honest they are | Distrust | Tell for overcompensation |
| Partner candidate is cynical/pessimistic | Pass | Beliefs are self-fulfilling |
| Can't see working with someone for life | Don't work with them for a day | Relationships compound |
| Business partner thinks short-term with others | Walk away | They'll do it to you |
| Someone is angry / conflict-prone | Cut them out | Anger is a hot coal |
| Job is trainable by society | Not wealth path | Trainable = replaceable |
| Work: rent time vs own equity | Prefer equity | Sleep-earning assets only |
| Leverage choice | Code/media over labor/capital | Permissionless, no gatekeeper |
| Reading a book that bores you | Skip/drop it, no obligation | Great books are numerous |
| Old problem (health, peace, family) | Read the ancients | Older solutions are better |
| New problem (tech, modern) | Read modern sources | Modern solutions |
| "I don't have time" | Translate: "not a priority" | Priorities get done |
| Saying "I'll try" | Recognize as cop-out | Commit externally or shrink goal |
| Envy of someone | 100% swap test | No partial envy possible |
| Food decision | Less processed = better; avoid sugar+fat together | Sugar dominates fat, drives hunger |
| News makes you anxious/angry | Ignore; stay optimistic | Its job is engagement, trends are positive |
| Mood unstable | Drop alcohol, sugar, screens, caffeine | Long-term stability over short-term hit |
| Not present | Meditate 1 hr morning, 60 days | Mental fitness protocol |
| Overwhelmed by priorities | One #1 priority (health) | 10–15 priorities = none |
| Choosing values mismatch | Reconsider relationship | Values alignment dissolves little things |

## Trade-off Matrix — Leverage Types

| Leverage | Permission? | Scales? | Cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Labor | Permissioned | Poor (management mess) | You manage people | Worst form |
| Capital | Permissioned | Excellent | Must earn trust | 20th century's wealth |
| Code/Media | Permissionless | Infinite | Just your skill | New fortunes |
| Code + Media + Judgment | Permissionless | Infinite | Judgment required | Best combination |

## Thresholds & Defaults

- **Hourly rate**: set ~$1,000–5,000/hr aspirational; must feel absurdly high
- **Reading**: 1–2 hrs/day → top 0.00001% in ~7 years
- **Meditation**: 1 hour × 60 days to establish
- **Sleep**: 8–9 hrs, no alarms, circadian
- **Desires**: max 1 big at a time (2 = distraction)
- **Habits**: 6-month cadence of breaking/picking up; 10-year journey
- **Thinking space**: 1–2 empty days/week; ideas come after boredom
- **Big decisions**: spend 1–2 years on the 3 big ones
- **Decision quality**: 80% vs 70% right = hundreds-fold compensation with leverage
- **Effort**: 99% wasted — go all-in on the 1%
- **Compound check**: 30%/yr for 30 yrs = thousands of ×
- **Luck**: engineer kinds 3+4; you want wealthy in 999/1,000 universes

## Tells & Smells

- **Anti-wealth talk** ("money is evil") → status-game player attacking you
- **Fancy words, big concepts** → speaker doesn't know the basics
- **Neatly bundled beliefs** → unexamined identity, not thinking
- **Self-serving conclusions** → demand a higher bar
- **Desperation for money** → red flag, desperation smells
- **Jealousy** → you can't cherry-pick someone's life
- **Busy calendar, no empty space** → no thinking happening
- **"Should" in your thoughts** → guilt or social programming
- **Books you're "stuck" on** → obligation mindset; drop it and read something else
- **Anger** → loss of control; contract to suffer until reality changes
- **Endless upgrading lifestyle** → hedonic treadmill; hold fixed, take lump sums
- **Uncontrollable cravings** → the sugar/fat pairing is engineered, not willpower
