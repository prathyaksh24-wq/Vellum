# Glossary — The Almanack of Naval Ravikant

- **Accountability** — Taking business risks under your own name; society rewards it with responsibility, equity, and leverage (Ch 1)
- **Agent** — Someone working on another's behalf; optimizes for themselves rather than the principal's assets (Ch 2)
- **Anger** — A contract with yourself to be in turmoil until reality changes; a precursor to violence and a loss of control (Ch 4)
- **Black swans** — Extreme-probability tail events (Taleb); a core lens for risk (Ch 2)
- **Choiceless Awareness** — Nonjudgmental meditation: accept each moment without judging anything (Ch 4)
- **Compound interest** — Returns that multiply on themselves; applies to wealth, relationships, reputation, and knowledge (Ch 1, 2)
- **Contrarian** — One who reasons independently from the ground up and resists pressure to conform; not one who always objects (Ch 2)
- **Desire** — A contract you make with yourself to be unhappy until you get what you want (Ch 3)
- **Equity** — Ownership of a business; the only path to financial freedom and nonlinear earning (Ch 1)
- **Falsifiability** — The test of science: a claim must make falsifiable predictions and have predictive power (Ch 2, 5)
- **Five Chimps Theory** — You can predict a chimp by the five chimps it hangs out with most; humans too (Ch 3)
- **Freedom from** — Naval's evolved value: freedom from reaction, anger, sadness, and being forced to do things (Ch 4)
- **Freedom to** — The young Naval's value: freedom to do anything, anytime (Ch 4)
- **Fundamental delusion** — "There is something out there that will make me happy and fulfilled forever" (Ch 3)
- **Hedonic adaptation** — Quickly getting used to anything; stronger for man-made things than natural things (Ch 3)
- **Hedonic treadmill** — The endless lifestyle upgrade as income rises; the lust for money is a bottomless pit (Ch 1)
- **Hygiene hypothesis** — Sterile environments lead to allergies and untrained immune systems (Ch 4)
- **Inversion** — Being successful is about avoiding incorrect judgments, not having correct ones (Ch 2)
- **Judgment** — Wisdom applied to external problems; knowing long-term consequences and choosing well (Ch 2)
- **Leverage** — Force multiplier for judgment; comes from labor, capital, or products with no marginal cost of replication (code/media) (Ch 1)
- **Long-term games** — Iterated positive-sum games where compound interest rewards all players (Ch 1)
- **Monkey mind** — The always-worried, frightened, anxious layer of thought; a tool to control, not a master (Ch 4)
- **Money** — Social credits; an IOU from society for past value; how we transfer time and wealth (Ch 1)
- **Nexting** — Sitting in one spot thinking about where you should be next; the source of pervasive anxiety (Ch 3)
- **Permissionless leverage** — Code and media: leverage no one must grant you (Ch 1)
- **Permissioned leverage** — Capital and labor: someone must give them to you (Ch 1)
- **Principal** — The owner; cares and does a great job; attracts trust (Ch 2)
- **Principal-agent problem** — The most fundamental microeconomics problem; tie compensation to value created to fix it (Ch 2)
- **Productize Yourself** — "Yourself" = uniqueness + accountability + specific knowledge; "Productize" = leverage + scaling (Ch 1)
- **Radical honesty** — Say what you think and think what you say; the easiest person to fool is you (Ch 2)
- **Rational Buddhism** — Buddhism's internal work, reconciled with science and evolution; keep only what you can verify (Ch 5)
- **Real résumé** — A catalog of all your suffering — the hard things you chose to do (Ch 1)
- **Retirement** — When you stop sacrificing today for an imaginary tomorrow; when today is complete in itself (Ch 1)
- **Risk of ruin** — Jail, physical danger, or losing all capital; avoid, and take rationally optimistic bets instead (Ch 1)
- **Run uphill** — When evenly split, choose the path with more short-term pain; it carries the long-term gain (Ch 2)
- **Specific knowledge** — Knowledge you cannot be trained for; found in genuine curiosity, feels like play, can't be outsourced (Ch 1)
- **Status** — Your ranking in the social hierarchy; a zero-sum game (Ch 1)
- **Suffering** — The moment when you can no longer deny reality; a moment of truth (Ch 2)
- **Systems, not goals** — Create environments where you're statistically likely to succeed (Scott Adams) (Ch 4)
- **The 3 Big Decisions** — Where you live, who you're with, what you do; dominate your life trajectory (Ch 1)
- **The 3 Options** — Change it, accept it, or leave it; never wish without acting (Ch 3)
- **The 4 Kinds of Luck** — Blind; through hustle/motion; through preparedness; through unique character (Ch 1)
- **The trifecta** — Time, health, and money; get all three at once before it's too late (Ch 3)
- **Wealth** — Assets that earn while you sleep; the factory cranking out things (Ch 1)
- **Wisdom** — Knowing the long-term consequences of your actions; discarding vices and returning to virtue (Ch 2, 5)
