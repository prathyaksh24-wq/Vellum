## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Wealth (Ch 1)
- **Wealth = assets that earn while you sleep**; money is just an IOU transferring it. You won't get rich renting out your time — own equity in a business.
- **Specific knowledge** is what you cannot be trained for; it's found in your childhood inclinations and passions, feels like play, and can't be outsourced or automated. If society can train you, it can replace you.
- **Leverage is a force multiplier for judgment.** Three classes: labor (worst — permissioned, messy), capital (permissioned — 20th century's fortune-maker), and code/media — products with zero marginal cost of replication (permissionless — the source of all new fortunes).
- **Apply specific knowledge + accountability + leverage, and compound it over decades.** Productize Yourself = "yourself" (uniqueness, accountability) + "productize" (leverage, scaling).
- **Play long-term games with long-term people.** All returns — wealth, relationships, knowledge, reputation — come from compound interest. Pick partners with intelligence, energy, and above all integrity.
- **Set an aspirational hourly rate that feels absurdly high**; outsource or ignore anything below it.
- **Engineer luck kinds 3 and 4**: become so good that you spot breaks others miss, and so reputable that opportunity seeks you out. You want to be wealthy in 999 of 1,000 parallel universes.
- **Avoid the risk of ruin** (jail, health, total capital loss); take rationally optimistic bets with big upsides.
- **The 3 big decisions** (where you live, who you're with, what you do) deserve 1–2 years of deliberation each.

### Judgment (Ch 2)
- **Judgment = wisdom applied**; wisdom = knowing the long-term consequences of your actions. One correct decision wins everything in an age of leverage; direction beats speed.
- **Clear thinking is basic thinking**: if you can't explain it to a child, you don't know it. Nail fundamentals you can rederive — advanced concepts are less proven.
- **What we wish to be true clouds what is true.** Suffering is the moment you can no longer deny reality — and a moment of truth. Shrink the ego; acknowledge problems publicly.
- **Shed your identity**: package beliefs (Democrat, Catholic, libertarian) are suspect; re-evaluate from base principles.
- **Radical honesty**: you are the easiest person to fool (Feynman). Praise specifically, criticize generally (Buffett).
- **If you can't decide, the answer is no** — options are abundant, decisions are long-lived. If evenly split, **run uphill**: take the path with more short-term pain.
- **Collect mental models** — evolution, game theory, microeconomics, complexity, falsifiability, inversion, compound interest — as pointers to your own knowledge.
- **Master the principal-agent problem**: tie compensation to value created; be a principal, not an agent.

### Happiness (Ch 3)
- **Happiness is a skill and a choice** — learnable like fitness. Happiness = the state when nothing is missing; remove desire, get presence.
- **Desire is a contract with yourself to be unhappy until you get what you want.** Keep at most one big desire at a time; perfect your desires.
- **Choose peace over joy**: peace is happiness at rest; a happy person doesn't lose innate peace. Reality is neutral — your interpretation is the choice.
- **Every situation: change it, accept it, or leave it.** Never wish without acting — that struggle is most misery. Train the positive-interpretation reflex until sub-second.
- **Kill envy with the 100% swap test** — if you wouldn't swap lives wholesale, jealousy is pointless.
- **Happiness is built by habits**: surround yourself with the right five chimps; no screens for happiness; serotonin from sunlight, exercise, positive thinking.

### Saving Yourself (Ch 4)
- **Save yourself** — no expert will do it for you. Health (physical → mental → spiritual) is the #1 priority; peace of body first, then peace of mind.
- **Diet**: fat drives satiety, sugar drives hunger, and sugar dominates — avoid the sugar+fat combo; the more processed, the less you consume.
- **Exercise daily — anything.** "Easy choices, hard life. Hard choices, easy life" (Gregorek).
- **Meditation is intermittent fasting for the mind**: 1 hour × 60 days to protocol; mind is an inbox to reach zero. **Choiceless Awareness** is the recommended form.
- **The mind is a servant, not a master** — run it in debug mode; you are more than your monkey mind.
- **"Try" is a cop-out** — commit externally or set a smaller honest goal. **Systems, not goals** (Adams).
- **Move from freedom to → freedom from** (reaction, anger, expectations, employment).

### Philosophy (Ch 5)
- **Rational Buddhism**: evolution on one pole, Buddhism's internal work on the other — keep what you can verify, discard the rest. Falsifiability is the truth filter.
- **Meaning is personal and self-created**: the question matters more than the answer; life is a fun game — the present is all we have.
- **Live by non-negotiable values** (honesty, no short-term dealing, peers only, no anger); values alignment predicts relationship success.
- **The older the question, the older the answers** — read the ancients for ancient problems.

### The Compressed System (Ch 6)
- **Life Formulas**: Happiness = Health + Wealth + Good Relationships; Health = Exercise + Diet + Sleep; Wealth = Income + Wealth×ROI; Income = Accountability + Leverage + Specific Knowledge.
- **Rules**: health, love, and your mission, in that order — nothing else matters. Reading is the meta-skill, tradable for anything else.
