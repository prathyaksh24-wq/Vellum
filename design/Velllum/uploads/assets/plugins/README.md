# Plugin logos

Publisher logos are copied unchanged from the installed publisher bundles' declared
`interface.logo` (or `extensions.com.openai.interface.logo`) assets. The matching
publisher, public source, and bundle license are recorded in `publishers.json`.
Logo and trademark rights remain with their owners; bundle licenses do not grant
additional trademark rights.

Service SVGs use the actual brand marks distributed by Simple Icons, downloaded
from `https://cdn.simpleicons.org/<slug>` on 2026-10-17. They cover Airtable, Asana,
Box, Brave, Cloudflare, Discord, Dropbox, Figma, Gmail, Google Calendar, HubSpot,
Jira, MongoDB, Obsidian, PostgreSQL, Product Hunt, Puppeteer, Sentry, Shopify,
Spotify, Stripe, Supabase, Todoist, Vercel, X, and YouTube.
Slack's multicolor mark is copied from its official `a.slack-edge.com` marketing
asset already registered by Vellum.

Assets are served locally and share one icon frame in the plugin catalog. Dark
monochrome marks have a light presentation on dark backgrounds. Plugins without
a registered logo, and images that fail to load, use a neutral lettermark derived
from their name in the same frame. No substitute brand artwork is generated.
